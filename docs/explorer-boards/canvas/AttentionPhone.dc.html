Purpose

A rulebook maintainer opens this view to learn which rules need them now. It answers: what is out of date, unproven or past a date, and what do I do?

What it conveys, in order

  1. The eight kinds, each with how many rules it holds. The kind that needs two batches says so instead of showing a zero.
  2. Each rule: its id, its kind, why it is listed and the fix, in text.
  3. The rule's standing as a word and a symbol, and trial and unproven as marks.
  4. That the list is derived: nothing is typed into it.

How the layout carries that order

One column. The kinds wrap above the list as chips, then the rules as stacked rows.

Standing on every row

Every rule carries its standing as a word and a symbol; marks sit beside it. A kind is a word with a count, never colour alone.

Where it differs from the canvas, and why

  • The canvas had no attention board: it is new. It is drawn from S13 and its eight kinds, and the attention item for a rule a linter now holds that still sits in review text.
