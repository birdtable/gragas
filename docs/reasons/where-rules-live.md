# Where the rules live

Rules live in the rulebook and reach a project through its instructions and its template. The rules below depend on that arrangement.

- **agents.instructions.5** (Hard, active): Agents run in directories that are not sibling clones.
- **arch.decisions.3** (Review, active): A change settled by the wrong person is not settled; naming who settled it in the record is what lets a later reader trust it.
- **arch.templates.1** (Hard, active): A template that does not record its version cannot be updated, and a project that has drifted from it cannot be told apart from one that chose to.
